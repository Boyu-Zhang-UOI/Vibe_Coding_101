# Rescue starters

Small, working apps for students who have fallen behind, so you can keep taking part in the next
week's activities while you catch up. Each one is **a reference, not your project**: talk to your
instructor about how you'll finish your own work.

| Starter | What it is | Use it when |
|---|---|---|
| [Study Log](study-timer/) | A working study timer in plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript, built from the [week 3 example spec](../../weeks/03-spec-first/example-spec.md), with tested pure functions | Your Project 1 doesn't work by week 4 |

For the capstone, the [capstone starter](../capstone-starter/) is already a working app, with tested
patterns for each feature type. If your capstone stalls, go back to it and to your
[milestones](../capstone.md#milestones), and talk to your instructor early.

To copy a starter into a new repository, follow
[Start a project from a starter](../../setup/codespaces.md#start-a-project-from-a-starter) with the
starter's path, for example `projects/rescue-starters/study-timer`.
