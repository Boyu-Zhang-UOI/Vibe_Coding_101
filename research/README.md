# Research behind the course

Vibe Coding 101 was designed from a review, carried out in September 2026, of how other courses teach AI-assisted programming, what the evidence says about learning with AI, what goes wrong in vibe-coded software, and which tools are free. An update in October 2026 re-checked every tool and added the studies, incidents and courses published in between; together with a review of the course itself, it shaped the October 2026 revision.

## How to read the notes

- Every fact carries a link and, where available, a date. Facts about tools were checked on **25 September 2026** (September notes) and **21 October 2026** (October notes) and will go stale. The course keeps current numbers only in [TOOLS.md](../TOOLS.md).
- Notes mark third-party or search-snippet sources and flag conflicting sources. The reports' "gaps" paragraphs list what could not be verified.
- The notes were compiled with AI research assistants and then synthesized. Treat them as a well-sourced starting point, and follow the links before relying on any single claim.
