# Reflection — Week <N>

> 🔴 **Write this yourself, without AI.** 150–300 words. It is graded on specifics, not polish.
> Anchor it in concrete moments: a prompt, an error message, a line of code, a decision.

## 1. A moment that surprised me

When did the AI (or your code) do something you did not expect? What exactly happened, and what did you do next?

## 2. What I understand now that I didn't before

Explain one idea from this week as if to a friend who missed class.

## 3. What I still don't understand

Be honest. This is the most useful part for your instructor.

## 4. Confidence check

On a scale of 1–5, how confident are you that you could explain every part of what you submitted this week? ☐1 ☐2 ☐3 ☐4 ☐5
Now pick the part you are *least* sure about and explain it here in two sentences. Did your explanation match your rating?
